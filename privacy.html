Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how Billionade ("we", "us") and its internal tool Billionade Studio handle information. Billionade is a YouTube channel that publishes stories about brands and businesses.

Billionade Studio and YouTube API Services

Billionade Studio is a private tool used only by the Billionade team to manage our own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services to read our channel information and analytics and to manage our own videos, playlists and metadata. By using it, we agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • We access only data of our own channel, with our own authorization.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of YouTube viewers or other users.
  • Access tokens are stored securely and used only to operate our channel. You can revoke access granted to Billionade Studio at any time from the Google security settings page.
  • Channel analytics are used in aggregate only, to improve our content.

Website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal information. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request.

Social media

When you interact with us on YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, those platforms' own privacy policies apply.

Children

Our content is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

For any question about this policy, contact us through the contact email listed on our YouTube channel page (youtube.com/@TheBillionade).
